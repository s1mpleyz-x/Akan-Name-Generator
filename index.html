Akan Name Generator

The Akan people of Ghana, Ivory Coast, and Togo frequently name their children after the day of the week they were born on and the order in which they were born. These "day names" have further meanings surrounding the soul and character of the person. Middle names have considerably more variety and can refer to their birth order, twin status, or an ancestor's middle name.
