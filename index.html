Akan Name Generator

Among the Akan people of Ghana, it is a tradition to name their children based on the weekday they were born on and their birth sequence. These "day names" have deeper meanings surrounding the person's soul, spirit and character. Middle names show much greater diversity, often reflecting birth order, whether the child is a twin, or the middle name of a family ancestor.
